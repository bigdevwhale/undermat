## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- A noticeboard of twelve flats for a week: Naples, Kyoto, Oaxaca, Bergen, Seoul, Lyon, Tbilisi, Kolkata, Salvador, Lisbon, Galway, Istanbul, each with its own host.
- The doormat, the key and the host's note under it.
- Each flat drawn in code, with its city's floor, tiles, window and one object of its own; the view is the host's city at its real local time, sunrise, sunset and weather.
- Seven days of notes per host, one new day each morning: a dish with substitutes and a shopping list, a film, a record, a book, a fact and a word of the day said aloud, a house rule, a letter, an untranslatable word, the window and the host's holidays.
- Neighbours dropping by on days 2, 4 and 6; an evening card for two or for friends.
- The suitcase of stickers, the gift left on day seven, "My weeks" to read a past week again.
- A quiet evening note when the next day's letters are in.
- English and Russian.
